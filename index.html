Using an iPhone?

Apple requires this Call Alerts page to be added to your Home Screen before push notifications can be enabled.

  1. Open this page on your iPhone in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Kasselmann Call Alerts from your Home Screen.
  5. Tap Enable Push Notifications.
